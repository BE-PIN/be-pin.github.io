# Appendix B — Information needs by decision and epidemic phase

Results of the BE-PIN WP1 national survey and consultation (**Section 8.3.2** of the main report).

## How to read this appendix

Information needs are grouped by decision, and decisions by domain. For each decision, **n** is the number of respondents who answered for that decision. The figure in each epidemic-phase column is the number of those respondents who considered the information need essential at that phase, so it ranges from 0 to n. Each figure carries a shading block reproducing the heat scale of the source table, so denser blocks mark stronger agreement:

| Shading | · | ░ | ▒ | ▓ | █ |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Respondents** | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
