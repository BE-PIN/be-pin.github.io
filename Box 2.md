> ### Box 2. Data roles and definitions (see Art. 2 EHDS and Art. 4 GDPR for detailed definitions). 
>
> In the sense of European Health Data Space (EHDS), the **data holder** is the entity responsible for maintaining, storing, protecting, and documenting the data. 
>
> The **data requester** is the entity that seeks access to data held by a data holder.
>
> **Data user** is the entity that actually uses the shared data for analysis, research, policy-making, product development, or other approved purposes. The data user may or may not be the same as the data requester (e.g., a requester might obtain data for use by a wider research consortium).
>
> **Data processor** and **data controller** both occur in the context of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), i.e., involving processing of **personal data**. The controller is the entity deciding on the purposes and means for processing personal data and is primarily responsible for compliance with GDPR. Personal data are any information which are related to an identified or identifiable natural person. A processor acts on behalf of a controller in cases when the controller does not process the data itself. Multiple entities can also be joint-controllers determining purposes and means jointly and both being responsible for GDPR compliance.
