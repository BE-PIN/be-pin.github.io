# Appendix A — Scenario typology

Mapping of the five epidemic phases to their operational response stages and example response targets. Referenced in **Section 3** of the main report.

| Epidemic phase | Operational stage(s) | Example response targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-pandemic | Prepare; Prevent | Surveillance and laboratory capacity; pathogen circulation and population vulnerability |
| First 100 cases | Respond: Get ready; Respond: Contain | Detection and surge capacity; early transmission dynamics and spatial spread |
| Exponential phase | Respond: Scale up; Control/Reduce; Mitigate | Intervention and surveillance scale-up; health-system pressure, supply constraints, societal impact |
| Control and stabilisation | Recover: Scale down; Recover: Sustain | Intervention adjustment; health-system, surveillance and supply resilience; societal recovery |
| Preparation for future pandemics | Adjust; Strengthen | Future risk monitoring; analytical, laboratory and community preparedness |
