# Appendix 8.1 — Overview of the activities


- **Stakeholder meeting (March 2024):** presentation and validation of the draft epidemic scenarios at a national meeting organised by the Federal Public Service for Health (~50 participants), followed by bilateral consultations.
- **National survey (July 2025):** online survey built on the initial schema, sent to 155 stakeholders, assessing the importance of information needs across epidemic phases.
- **Two consultation workshops (14 and 22 January 2026, Brussels):** facilitated thematic working groups and plenary consolidation to prioritise and validate information needs.
- **Collaborative surveillance framework consolidation:** reorganisation of the prioritised needs into three layers and six operational domains with cross-cutting enabling elements.
- **Evidence-generation matrix construction:** linking epidemic phases and operational targets to information and data needs, with an accompanying interactive tool.
- **Data-need identification and reworking:** translation of information needs into granular data needs and reformatting for dataset mapping (Task 1.3).
- **Information- and data-need remapping exercise:** mapping of survey items to prioritised information needs and associated data needs.
- **Metadata database describing data sources:** compilation of metadata information on various datasets relevant for pandemic intelligence.
- **Dataset existence and accessibility mapping (Task 1.3):** mapping of data needs to existing datasets to identify gaps (ongoing).
- **Dataset quality deep-dives (partial):** completeness and quality (FAIR) assessment of datasets linked to foundational information needs (ongoing).
- **Data governance for sharing process assessment:** evaluation of current hurdles for timely and responsible data sharing of health data in Belgium and identification of potential solutions, including a consultation with the HDA.
- **International perspective on data data sharing (Task 1.2)**
