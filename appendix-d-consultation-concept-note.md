# Appendix D — Information needs consultation: concept note

*BE-PIN WP1 Information Needs Consultation – Concept Note (Full-Day)*

Concept note for the BE-PIN WP1 full-day consultation on information needs (**Section 8.3.2** of the main report). The consolidated outputs of this consultation are reported in [Appendix C](appendix-c-layered-surveillance-model.md).

## Background

As part of the Belgian Pandemic Intelligence Network (BE-PIN), Work Package 1 focuses on understanding and prioritising the information and data needs that support effective decision-making during epidemics and pandemics. The objective is to move beyond classic surveillance indicators and better align data, analysis, and intelligence with the real needs of decision-makers across the Belgian federal system.

This consultation builds on preparatory work carried out within BE-PIN, including a national survey launched in July 2025. Particular attention has been given to ensuring balanced representation across federal, regional, clinical, public health, and crisis-management levels, so that the discussion reflects the diversity of decision-making roles involved during epidemics.

## Purpose of the consultation

The consultation aims to:

- Identify and prioritise the essential information needs required for effective decision-making in the context of respiratory diseases with epidemic or pandemic potential.
- Validate and consolidate these information needs across different stakeholder perspectives.
- Produce a clear and coherent set of priority information needs to support subsequent data mapping, system development, and preparedness work within BE-PIN.
- Capture diverse operational, clinical, and policy perspectives through a structured and transparent process.

## Structure of the day

### Morning session — Identification and prioritisation of information needs (small groups)

In the morning, participants will work in small, mixed groups on a subset of predefined decision areas (e.g. surveillance, clinical response, prevention and control).

For each decision:

- Participants will first reflect individually on what information they consider essential to make the decision responsibly.
- This will be followed by a structured group discussion to identify and prioritise the essential information needs.
- A predefined list of information needs derived from prior work will be available as a prompt to help identify possible blind spots, but will not be treated as a checklist.

The morning session will result in a preliminary set of priority information needs identified by each group.

### Afternoon session — Plenary validation and consolidation

In the afternoon, all participants will come together to review the priority information needs identified by the different groups during the morning session.

The plenary review will be used to:

- Check the completeness and relevance of the proposed priority information needs.
- Clarify and refine the wording of information needs where necessary.
- Identify and merge overlapping or duplicate information needs.
- Remove items considered unclear, insufficiently relevant, or not appropriate as distinct information needs.
- Agree on a consolidated set of priority information needs across the consultation groups.

This final plenary step ensures that the outputs reflect not only the priorities identified within individual groups, but also collective review and validation across the different operational, clinical, public health, and policy perspectives represented in the consultation.

## Outputs

The consultation will produce:

- A consolidated and collectively reviewed set of priority information needs for the decisions covered.
- A record of information needs refined, merged, or removed through the plenary validation process.
- Inputs for subsequent mapping of information needs to data needs and available datasets within BE-PIN WP1.
